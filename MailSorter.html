About

The case

A university gets timetables, announcements and newsletters mixed with phishing and prize scams. A single word like "urgent" cannot decide, so the model learns word combinations from labelled emails.

From the literature

Heliyon review (Dada et al., 2019): judge filters on more than accuracy, treat false positives as costly, and expect spam to drift over time.

Limits

The filter supports human review and does not replace it. Spam wording changes, so retrain on fresh labelled data.
